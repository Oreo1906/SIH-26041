# Third-party visual assets

No third-party 3D models, textures or audio have been imported yet. The preview
uses generated primitive geometry.
Runtime package dependencies are listed in backend/admin lockfiles; a complete
third-party software notices bundle is still required before release.

User preference: use suitable open-source prebuilt 3D models when useful; choose
primitives when simpler. Before adding an asset, record its name, author, source
URL, exact version/download date, redistribution license, bundled license path,
modifications, and destination file(s). Include attribution required by its license.

Bundle worker assets locally; never require runtime CDN/download access. Favor
small mobile-friendly assets and verify performance on the target Android device.
Scene visuals must consume the same renderer-independent training engine.
Asset appearance does not establish approved safety rules or SOPs.

## Original procedural scene models (2026-09-30)

Mine supports/rock, extinguisher, alarm, detector, safety rack, attendant, barriers,
exit signs and stylized fire are original geometry generated by
`Presentation/TrainingGeometry.cs`. No external 3D asset or texture was copied.
All models are generated offline from source and reuse a small material palette.
Unity primitives are used as building blocks. This keeps the APK compact and
allows the same geometry to render in simulation and AR.

## Optional AR packages (2026-09-30)

Unity AR Foundation6.3.5, ARCore6.3.5, XR Management4.6.1 and Core Utils2.6.0 are
pinned in `Packages/manifest.json` / `packages-lock.json`. Package `LICENSE.md`
and third-party notices ship in the editor's package distribution. They are Unity
packages, not claimed as original or open-source third-party 3D models. Worker
simulation does not require an AR service or network. AR requires its device
service to have been installed previously; the app never initiates its download.
